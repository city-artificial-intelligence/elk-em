Gene Ontology (go.owl, go.norm, go.normalized.owl, gci0_ontology.owl, bins/,
DELEclosure/)

Michael Ashburner, Catherine A Ball, Judith A Blake, David Botstein, Heather
Butler, J Michael Cherry, Allan P Davis, Kara Dolinski, Selina S Dwight, Janan T
Eppig, et al. 2000. Gene ontology: tool for the unification of biology. Nature
genetics 25, 1 (2000), 25-29.

Source: https://geneontology.org/
Data is made available under the terms of the CC BY 4.0 license.
This is an adaption of the original work: the ontology has been normalised into
EL+bot normal forms and the resulting axioms split into bins.

---

Protein sequences (human_proteins.fasta, cafa5_proteins.txt) and derived
ESM-2 representations (esm2_480.h5)

Sequences originate from UniProtKB (https://www.uniprot.org/), available under
CC BY 4.0. Representations were computed with ESM-2 (esm2_t12_35M_UR50D),
released by Meta AI under the MIT license:
Zeming Lin, Halil Akin, Roshan Rao, Brian Hie, et al. 2023. Evolutionary-scale
prediction of atomic-level protein structure with a language model. Science
379, 6637 (2023), 1123-1130.
